## General remarks

### Koen Hufkens

Throughout there is a poor understanding of the structure of a project, the 
setting of paths, the use of github and the tasks at hand.

I think there is a general sense of inability to focus on 'what is
important'. In short, they have to do 'Everything Everywhere All at Once', this
trips up many. IMO we get the order of things wrong, and data and code
management should be moved to the front, before even considering doing 'fun'
stuff. Most of them won't consider it 'fun' if they are lost otherwise.

There is a strong need for intermittent evaluation of the projects (on github),
cursory evaluation would show shortcomings and students falling behind. I would,
in this context, not allow any students to follow the course 'remotely'. If still
having a remote option, they should play by the same rules (report intermittently
and interact remotely via github) or face the consequences (bad grades without 
exception). The no office hour and questions policy was in part the undoing here.
